The gap the
CCA exam left.

The CCA exam mentions "pollination" once. There is no advisor-level framework for pollination timing, colony specifications, bloom-window spray decisions, or what to do when fruit set falls short.

Growers spend on bees and services without a qualified professional managing the outcome. APIS fills that gap, with the decision-making framework a crop adviser needs to manage pollination as a professional input.

The seven modules

Each module is self-directed and takes 55–65 minutes, including a 10-question knowledge check. Minimum passing score is 75%.

01
Module 1

Pollination as a Managed Input

Reframe pollination from background condition to managed agronomic input. Field evidence from almonds, blueberries, and strawberries shows what unmanaged pollination costs — and what a qualified recommendation changes.

~60 min
02
Module 2

Applied Pollinator Biology

What an advisor needs to know about colony dynamics, foraging behavior, and native species — without becoming a beekeeper. Covers how weather, crop stage, and landscape context shape what pollinators do in a field.

~60 min
03
Module 3

Pollination Requirements Across Crop Systems

A working framework for assessing any crop's pollination needs — from self-compatible to obligate cross-pollinators. Bloom window mapping, hive density and placement principles, and how to read fruit set as diagnostic feedback.

~60 min
04
Module 4

Writing a Pollination Management Recommendation

How to structure and document a complete pollination plan — timing, colony specifications, placement, contingencies. Covers the documentation standards that make a recommendation professionally defensible, not just plausible.

~60 min
05
Module 5

Pesticide Decisions Before and During Bloom

The highest-liability intersection in ag advising. Covers toxicity classification, label law under FIFRA, bloom-window pesticide calendars, and the communication protocols and documentation that protect the advisor when decisions are questioned.

~60 min
06
Module 6

In-Season Management and Troubleshooting

What to do when conditions don't match the plan. Weather-driven adjustments, identifying under-pollination during bloom, mid-season hive corrections, escalation protocols, and the post-season debrief that makes next year's plan better.

~60 min
07
Module 7

Field Data, Regional Insights, and Professional Contribution

How to collect and submit standardized field observations through APIS Reports, how regional data strengthens your client recommendations, and how data contribution feeds back into credential renewal. The capstone module.

~60 min

Who this is designed for

Good fit
  • Certified Crop Advisers (CCAs) advising on specialty or perennial crops
  • PCAs working with growers who use managed pollinators
  • Working agronomists who want to add a defensible pollination recommendation to their practice
  • Consultants advising growers who have experienced fruit set shortfalls
Not designed for
  • Beekeepers or apiary managers: this is crop advising, not hive management
  • Conservation or habitat-focused practitioners: that is a different discipline
  • Row-crop or commodity advisors without perennial or specialty crop clients
  • Growers looking for a farm-level certification (see APIS Certified Farm)
