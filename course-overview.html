What the course covers

Seven core modules

Practical, science-based training for advisors who make the calls growers act on during bloom.

01

Pollination as a managed input

Why pollination gets overlooked, why it should be managed like any other farm input, and what that looks like across a variety of cropping systems.

02

Applied pollinator biology

Colony structure, foraging force and the limits on what bees can do. How to assess a hive without opening it, and which common assumptions are wrong.

03

Pollination requirements across crop systems

Crop requirements, bloom-window mapping, stocking density and placement, and how to tell whether pollination was the cause of poor fruit set.

04

Writing a pollination management recommendation

The three-phase recommendation document, minimum documentation, and how to make a defensible recommendation.

05

Pesticide decisions during bloom

Hazard, exposure and label restrictions, building a bloom-window spray calendar, and the communication chain.

06

In-season management and troubleshooting

Adjusting for marginal weather, diagnosing under-pollination, the escalation chain, and running a season debrief that feeds next year's plan.

07

Field data, APIS Reports and professional contribution

Data collection standards, going from observation to report, the APIS ToolKit, and how pooled field records build industry benchmarks.

Crop modules

Go deeper on the crops you advise: almonds, blueberries, cane berries, strawberries, controlled environments and more. $215 each, released crop by crop.
