Our Philosophy

Healthy pollination is great for your farming business. Most pollination certifications feel disconnected from that, focused on habitat and land set-asides rather than what actually happens on a working farm. APIS is different: it's built around the daily agronomic decisions that most directly affect pollination outcomes, spray timing, IPM practices, hive management, and water access. That makes certification accessible to growers who can't dedicate acreage to habitat, and focused on what actually drives your yield.

APIS certification recognizes what you already do to manage pollination, and offers an achievable roadmap for what's next.

Built for Flexibility: You Choose What Fits Your Farm

Every farm is unique, and budgets are real. Instead of a one-size-fits-all requirement, our program allows you to choose which practices to put in place across the categories weighted by their actual impact on pollinator health.

Applications: Timing, product choice, bee-friendly substitutions
IPM Practices: Threshold-based, not calendar-based, decisions
Hive Management: Placement, communication, entrance health
Clean Water Sources: Accessible, maintained water near bloom
Pollinator Habitat: Dedicated habitat and/or forage for pollinators
Alternative Pollinator Presence: Native bees, mason bees, or other pollinators beyond managed hives
