Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

APIS works with growers, beekeepers and crop advisors. This policy explains what we collect when you visit our site or work with us, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Effective January 2026

Who we are

APIS Institute LLC (Applied Pollination Institute of Science, "APIS", "we") is a pollination-science consultancy based in California. We operate apisinstitute.org and are responsible for the personal information described here.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. Your name, email, phone number, organization, location, crops and the details you include when you send an inquiry, book a call, join a waitlist or enroll in a course.
  • Engagement data. Field, orchard and hive information you share while we deliver consulting, assessments or certification.
  • Technical data. Standard information your browser sends when you visit, such as IP address, device and browser type, and pages viewed, collected by our hosting and analytics providers.

We do not collect payment card numbers on our site. Payments, where applicable, are handled by the payment provider.

How we use it

  • To answer your inquiries and schedule calls.
  • To deliver and invoice the services you request.
  • To run our certification programs and waitlists.
  • To send occasional updates about APIS, which you can opt out of at any time.
  • To keep the site working, secure and useful.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Your farm data

Data from your operation stays confidential. We do not publish or share information that identifies your farm, blocks or results without your written permission. Any use of de-identified or aggregated data in research follows the terms of your engagement agreement, which takes precedence over this policy where the two differ.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us operate, such as website hosting, email, scheduling and accounting, under obligations to protect it.
  • With education partners that host our courses, when you enroll through them.
  • With other parties when you ask us to, or with your consent.
  • When required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

Cookies and tracking

Our site may use cookies and similar tools to remember preferences and to understand traffic in aggregate. Third-party providers, such as analytics and embedded scheduling tools, may collect information about your activity on our site. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Do Not Track. Our site does not currently respond to browser Do Not Track signals, as there is no common standard for them.

Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, then delete or de-identify it. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you updates. Use the unsubscribe link in any email or contact us below. We will respond within a reasonable time and will not treat you differently for making a request.

California residents may have additional rights under state law, including the right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used.

Children

Our services are intended for agricultural professionals and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed. Material changes will be posted on this page.
