The Expert

Angelita De la Luz
PhD

I've studied pollination from every angle over the past decade, first as an ecologist tracking how plant-pollinator networks respond to stress, then leading applied research across 12 crops in 6 countries as the Global Pollination Expert for one of the most innovative ag biotech companies in the world.

I have advised on pollination management from family farms to global producers and built pollination management courses for two of the largest crop advising companies in the world. I currently provide pollination consulting services for leading berry and tree nut producers and am open to all consulting for crop types.

"I've watched growers spray for pests that weren't there, and invest in pollination products and services that went nowhere, when the real problem, poor pollination, went unmanaged. The true gap isn't bees, or technology. It's knowledge."

Background

  • Applied research across 12 crops in 6 countries spanning 77 production cycles
  • Former Global Pollination Expert at a leading agricultural biotech company
  • 150+ grower fields advised across berries, tree nuts, and row crops
  • Built pollination management curricula for two of the world's largest crop advising companies
  • Active consulting engagements with leading berry and tree nut producers
  • Advisory relationships spanning USDA/ARS, UC Davis, Washington State University, Driscoll's, Olam Food Ingredients, Fall Creek, Almond Board of California, and US Highbush Blueberry Council
