Sign up to be an APIS
research partner

We are in search of growers and beekeepers to partner with on independent field trials of new pollination and hive monitoring tools, technologies, and products. Join us for the 2027 season.

Who We're Looking For

  • Growers of almonds, blueberries and other pollinator-dependent crops
  • Beekeepers with commercial colonies who are open to trying new technology
  • Crop advisors who work with either and want to be involved

What You Get

  • A report on your own colonies or pollination from blocks included in any research trials
  • Early access to new pollination and hive monitoring tools, technologies, and products
  • Independent data and insights, designed and analyzed by APIS

What's Involved

  • Access for our team to your hives or orchard during the trial
  • We take the measurements and work around your operation
  • Details, timing and any devices are agreed with you in advance

APIS works with multiple technology partners. Every trial is designed and analyzed by APIS, and results are reported independently.
